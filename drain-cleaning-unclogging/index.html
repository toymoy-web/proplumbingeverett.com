Local Cause

Root Intrusion and Everett's Older Drain Lines

Everett's older neighborhoods, particularly around downtown and the established blocks near Grand Avenue and Rucker Hill, were built with narrower-diameter clay drain and sewer lines that predate modern PVC standards. Mature street trees planted decades ago have had plenty of time to find the joints in that clay pipe, and root intrusion is one of the most common reasons a drain that seemed fine for years suddenly starts backing up repeatedly.

Grease buildup is the other major local pattern, especially in kitchen lines feeding into the same narrower pipe, since grease that would move through a modern 4-inch PVC line without issue can accumulate and harden inside an older, smaller-diameter clay or cast-iron drain.

Fixture age compounds both problems. Older cast-iron drain stacks develop internal rust scale over decades, and that rough interior surface gives grease and debris something to cling to that a smooth PVC interior wouldn't. A line that's been slowly narrowing from scale buildup for years can go from slow-draining to fully blocked after one heavy-use event, like a holiday gathering or extended house guests.

Mature street trees and older clay laterals are the pattern we plan for in the established neighborhoods around downtown, including Bayside, Port Gardner, Glacier View, and Northwest Everett. South Everett's mix of homes and businesses along the Evergreen Way corridor, which carries Highway 99 through the city, adds restaurant and multi-unit lines to the mix, and grease is the usual culprit on those.

Emergency Response

When a Clogged Drain Becomes an Emergency

A slow drain can wait for a regular appointment, but a drain that backs up into a tub, shower or floor drain cannot. Sewage coming up in the lowest fixture of the house, several fixtures gurgling at once, or an overflowing toilet that will not clear all point to a blockage in the main line.

Stop running water, since every gallon you send down has nowhere to go. Call our 24/7 line and we will dispatch a technician who can clear the blockage and camera the line to see why it formed. If roots, a sag or a broken section caused it, we will explain the options before moving to repair.

Call the 24/7 line right away if

  • Water or sewage is coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain
  • Two or more fixtures back up or gurgle at the same time
  • A toilet overflows and will not clear with a plunger
  • The lowest drain in the house backs up after heavy use elsewhere
  • A restaurant or business drain stops working during open hours
Signs

Signs of a Clog Before It Becomes a Backup

  • Water draining slower than usual in a sink, tub, or shower.
  • Gurgling sounds from a drain or toilet when another fixture is used.
  • A recurring clog in the same drain that comes back weeks after snaking.
  • Sewage odor near a floor drain or basement fixture.
  • Multiple fixtures backing up at once, which usually points to a main line blockage, not a single fixture.
Method

How We Find the Actual Blockage

For a single slow fixture, a mechanical snake with a cutting head usually clears the immediate blockage. For a recurring clog or a suspected root intrusion issue, we run a camera inspection through the line first to see exactly what's causing the backup and where, rather than guessing based on symptoms alone.

A camera inspection also tells us whether the underlying pipe itself has a structural problem, a collapsed section, a belly where the pipe sags and traps debris, or root intrusion at a joint, which changes the recommended fix from a cleaning to a repair. This is the same diagnostic step we use before any sewer line repair quote.

Options

Snaking, Hydro Jetting, or a Camera-Guided Repair

A standard mechanical snake handles most single-fixture clogs from hair, grease, or minor debris. For a line with heavier buildup, root intrusion, or a recurring blockage, hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full interior diameter of the pipe rather than just punching a hole through the clog, which is why it lasts longer on root-affected lines.

If the camera inspection shows a structural problem, a cracked joint, or a collapsed section, cleaning the line won't solve it permanently. At that point we'll walk you through spot repair versus a longer term fix rather than repeating the same cleaning call every few months.

For homes with a documented root intrusion history, an annual maintenance cleaning before the rainy season adds real value even when the line isn't currently backing up. Roots grow more actively during the wetter months, and a preventive jetting pass often costs a fraction of an emergency call during an actual backup.

Kitchen Drains

Kitchen Sink Clogs and Slow Kitchen Drains

Kitchen drains clog differently than bathroom drains, because grease, food particles and soap residue build up as a sticky layer inside the pipe. The City of Everett's fats, oils and grease (FOG) materials say cooking oil and food waste poured down a drain build up and cause clogs, backups and sewer overflows.

A snake can clear a kitchen clog, but if the sink backs up again within weeks, the cause is usually buildup farther down the line, a shared branch with a dishwasher or disposal, or a pipe with a sag. A camera inspection shows which. If a disposal is part of the problem, see our garbage disposal repair page.

Pricing

What Affects Drain Cleaning Cost in Everett

Cost Factors

  • Access: a floor drain or main cleanout is faster to reach than a line behind a fixture.
  • Method: hydro jetting costs more than standard snaking but clears root intrusion more thoroughly.
  • Camera inspection: recommended for recurring clogs, adds diagnostic time but prevents repeat calls.
  • Line length and depth: a main sewer line takes longer to clear than a single fixture drain.
  • Severity: a fully backed-up line requiring emergency dispatch is priced differently than routine maintenance.

Drain backups that cause water damage inside the home are sometimes covered under homeowner's policies, but coverage varies significantly by carrier and by whether the backup originated inside the home or in the municipal line. Check your policy's sewer backup rider specifically.

Before You Call

What to Do Before We Arrive

  • Stop running water into the affected fixture to avoid making a backup worse.
  • Avoid chemical drain cleaners on a recurring clog, since they can obscure what a camera inspection finds.
  • Note which fixtures are affected, since multiple fixtures backing up together usually means a main line issue.
  • If sewage is backing up into a fixture, avoid using other drains in the house until we arrive.
  • Check whether the issue started after any recent yard or landscaping work near the drain line's path.
Questions

Drain Cleaning Questions

Why does my drain keep clogging in the same spot?

A recurring clog in the same location usually means the underlying pipe has a structural issue, commonly root intrusion in older Everett clay lines, rather than a simple debris blockage that snaking alone will permanently fix.

What's the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?

Snaking punches through a blockage with a cutting head. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the full interior of the pipe, which works better on grease buildup and root intrusion.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency drain cleaning?

Yes. A fully backed-up drain, especially one affecting multiple fixtures, is treated as an emergency call.

Will you recommend a camera inspection every time?

No, only for recurring clogs or suspected line damage. A single, isolated slow drain usually just needs standard snaking.

Can tree roots really grow into a sewer or drain line?

Yes. Roots seek out moisture and will grow through even a small crack or joint gap in clay pipe. Once inside, they expand and trap debris, which is why the same spot tends to clog again after simple cutting alone.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

In most cases, yes, when performed at the correct pressure for the pipe material and condition. We assess pipe condition via camera first on older lines to confirm jetting won't stress an already compromised section.

Do you serve areas outside Everett?

Yes, including Snohomish, Mukilteo, Lake Stevens, Mill Creek, Edmonds, Marysville, and Lynnwood.

Can you clear floor drains and outdoor drains?

Floor drains are a routine part of our drain cleaning work. For an outdoor yard drain or trench drain, describe the drain and where it runs when you call and we will confirm what we can do.
