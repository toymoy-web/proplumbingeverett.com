Hydro Jetting in Everett, WA

When standard snaking keeps clearing the same clog for a few weeks before it comes back, hydro jetting addresses the buildup that snaking leaves behind.

Call (425) 845-0257 See FAQs

A lot of Everett homeowners call us after a drain has already been snaked once or twice by another company, and the clog keeps returning within weeks. That pattern usually means the line has heavy grease buildup, scale, or root intrusion that a standard snake's cutting head punches through without actually clearing.

Hydro jetting uses a high-pressure water stream to scour the full interior diameter of the pipe, not just carve a channel through the blockage, which is why it typically lasts significantly longer on these recurring problems.

Local Cause

Why Hydro Jetting Matters More in Everett's Older Lines

Everett's older neighborhoods run narrower-diameter clay and cast-iron drain and sewer lines that accumulate buildup faster than modern PVC. Root intrusion at pipe joints is a documented, real issue in this city's older housing stock, and roots that have grown into a joint create a rough surface that traps debris even after the initial intrusion is cut back.

Grease buildup compounds this in kitchen lines specifically, since a narrower pipe with a rough interior surface (common in aging cast iron) gives hardened grease more surface area to cling to than a smooth modern pipe would.

Signs

When Hydro Jetting Is the Right Call

  • A drain that's been snaked before but clogs again within a few weeks to a few months.
  • Multiple slow drains throughout the house, suggesting a main line issue rather than one fixture.
  • A camera inspection showing visible root intrusion or heavy interior buildup.
  • Recurring kitchen drain clogs tied to grease accumulation.
  • A sewer line with a documented history of backups despite regular cleaning.
Method

Camera Inspection Before Jetting

We run a camera through the line before jetting whenever possible, both to confirm jetting is the right approach and to check the pipe's structural condition first. Jetting at high pressure against an already-compromised or cracked section of pipe can worsen a structural problem rather than solve the clog, so confirming pipe condition matters before starting.

For lines with confirmed root intrusion, we also note where the intrusion is occurring, since a single joint with recurring root growth may need a structural fix, not just repeated jetting every year.

Options

Hydro Jetting vs. Standard Snaking

Standard snaking remains the right choice for a simple, isolated clog from hair or minor debris, and it costs less. Hydro jetting is the better investment for grease buildup, root intrusion, or any drain with a documented pattern of returning after standard cleaning.

If a camera inspection reveals a structural defect rather than just buildup, we'll walk through sewer line repair options rather than repeating jetting on a line that has an underlying pipe problem jetting alone can't fix.

Pricing

What Affects Hydro Jetting Cost in Everett

Cost Factors

  • Line length and access: a main sewer line costs more to jet than a single fixture drain.
  • Camera inspection: recommended before jetting, adds diagnostic cost but confirms pipe condition first.
  • Severity of buildup: heavier root intrusion or grease accumulation takes longer to fully clear.
  • Pipe material and condition: older, more fragile pipe may need a more conservative pressure setting.
  • Frequency: a preventive annual jetting costs less than an emergency response to a full backup.

Hydro jetting is a maintenance and repair service, not typically covered under homeowner's insurance unless it's part of remediation for a covered water damage event.

Before You Call

What to Check Before You Call

  • Note whether this drain has been snaked before and how quickly the clog returned.
  • Check whether multiple drains are affected, which points to a main line rather than one fixture.
  • Avoid chemical drain cleaners, since they can complicate a camera inspection and are hard on older pipe.
  • If you've had a camera inspection already, have those findings ready when you call.
  • Note any recent tree work or root growth near the sewer line's path in your yard.
Questions

Hydro Jetting Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older Everett pipes?

In most cases, yes, when we confirm pipe condition via camera first and use an appropriate pressure setting. A pipe with active structural damage may need repair before jetting is appropriate.

How long does hydro jetting last compared to snaking?

Because jetting clears the full pipe interior rather than punching through a blockage, results typically last significantly longer, especially for grease and root intrusion issues.

Do you always recommend a camera inspection first?

For jetting, yes, in most cases, since it confirms both the cause and that the pipe can safely handle the pressure.

Can hydro jetting fix root intrusion permanently?

It clears the current growth, but roots can regrow at the same joint over time unless the underlying crack is repaired. See sewer line repair for permanent fixes.

Do you serve areas outside Everett?

Yes, including Snohomish, Mukilteo, Lake Stevens, Mill Creek, and Edmonds.
