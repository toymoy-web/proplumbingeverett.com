Gas Line Installation & Repair in Everett, WA

Gas line work carries real safety stakes, and Everett's older homes sometimes have gas infrastructure that predates current inspection and permitting standards.

Call (425) 845-0257 See FAQs

Gas line installation and repair covers new gas lines for appliances like ranges, dryers, and outdoor fire features, as well as repairing an existing line showing signs of a leak or corrosion. This work follows Washington State licensing and permitting requirements specifically because the safety margin for error is much smaller than most other plumbing work.

If you smell gas anywhere near a line, appliance connection, or meter, that's not a diagnostic question for us to answer by phone. Leave the house immediately and call from outside before calling us or the gas utility.

Local Cause

Gas Line Age and Permitting in Older Everett Homes

Homes built before current code standards sometimes have gas lines installed without permits for later appliance additions, like a gas range swapped in without inspection, or an outdoor fire feature added without proper line sizing. We check for and correct these gaps as part of any new gas line work.

Older black iron gas pipe can also develop corrosion at threaded joints over many decades, which is a real safety consideration distinct from the newer flexible gas connector lines used in modern installations.

Signs

Signs of a Gas Line Problem

  • A sulfur or rotten-egg smell near an appliance, line, or meter.
  • A hissing sound near a gas connection or line.
  • Dead or discolored vegetation in a specific patch above a buried gas line.
  • An appliance with an unusually weak or yellow (rather than blue) flame.
  • Physical damage or visible corrosion on exposed gas piping.
Method

Leak Testing and Line Assessment

For a suspected leak, we use gas leak detection equipment to pinpoint the exact source at a connection, fitting, or a section of pipe, rather than assuming based on where the smell seems strongest, since gas can travel along a pipe before becoming detectable at the surface.

For new installations, we calculate proper line sizing based on total appliance demand, since an undersized line is a real performance and safety issue that isn't always obvious until an appliance underperforms or shuts off intermittently.

Options

Repair, New Installation, or Line Upgrade

A leak at a specific fitting or connection is typically a direct repair. Corroded black iron pipe sections are replaced rather than patched, since patching corroded gas pipe isn't a safe long-term fix.

For new appliance installations, we run a properly sized and permitted line rather than tapping into an existing line that wasn't sized for the additional demand, which is a common shortcut that causes problems later.

A gas water heater conversion is one of the most common reasons we run new gas line work in Everett. See our water heater installation & replacement page if you're considering switching fuel types during a replacement. For restaurants and other commercial properties, gas line sizing for kitchen equipment follows the same demand-calculation approach at a larger scale.

Pricing

What Affects Gas Line Cost in Everett

Cost Factors

  • Scope: a single appliance connection costs less than a new line run for an outdoor feature.
  • Line length and routing: longer runs or ones requiring wall or slab penetration cost more.
  • Pipe material: repairs to older black iron sometimes require broader section replacement.
  • Permits: Everett requires permitting and inspection for gas line work.
  • Appliance demand: line sizing for higher-demand appliances affects material cost.

Gas line repair due to age or wear is a maintenance expense. Damage from a gas-related incident may be covered separately under your homeowner's policy; check your specific coverage.

Before You Call

What to Do If You Smell Gas

  • Leave the house immediately if you smell gas anywhere near a line, appliance, or meter.
  • Do not operate light switches, phones, or anything that could create a spark inside the house.
  • Call from outside the house, either us or the gas utility's emergency line.
  • Do not attempt to locate or fix a suspected gas leak yourself.
  • Wait for a professional to confirm it's safe before re-entering.
Questions

Gas Line Questions

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the house immediately and call from outside. Do not use light switches or anything that could spark. See the checklist above for full steps.

Do I need a permit for a new gas line?

Yes, Everett requires permitting and inspection for gas line installation and most repair work.

Can you add a gas line for an outdoor fire feature or grill?

Yes, including proper line sizing and permitting for outdoor gas installations.

Is older black iron gas pipe dangerous?

Not inherently, but corroded sections should be assessed and replaced rather than left in place. We check joint condition during any gas line service call.

Do you serve areas outside Everett?

Yes, including Snohomish, Mukilteo, Lake Stevens, Mill Creek, and Edmonds.
