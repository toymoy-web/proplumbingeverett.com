Sewer Line Replacement in Everett, WA

When a sewer lateral has multiple failure points or a significant belly, replacement is the more durable fix than repeated spot repairs.

Call (425) 845-0257 See FAQs

Full sewer line replacement is a different job from a sewer line repair, and the distinction matters for both cost and outcome. A repair addresses one or two isolated problems on an otherwise sound line. Replacement is the right call when a camera inspection shows a lateral that's failing in multiple places, has a significant belly, or has simply reached the end of its usable life.

Everett's older clay sewer laterals, many now well past their original 50-70 year design life, are the most common candidates for full replacement rather than another round of spot repairs.

Local Cause

When Repeated Spot Repairs Stop Making Sense

A clay lateral with root intrusion at one joint is usually a repair candidate. A line with root intrusion recurring at multiple joints, a section that's collapsed, or a belly where the pipe has sagged and traps debris regardless of root activity, has moved past what spot repair can permanently fix.

We see this pattern most often in Everett's oldest neighborhoods, where the original clay lateral was installed before 1970 and has had five or more decades of soil settling, root pressure, and normal wear working against it.

Signs

Signs You Need Full Replacement, Not Repair

  • A camera inspection showing multiple separate failure points along the line.
  • Repeated backups despite prior spot repairs at different locations.
  • A confirmed significant belly or sag in the pipe.
  • A line original to a home built before 1970 with no prior replacement history.
  • Persistent sewage odor or lawn saturation along the full length of the lateral.
Method

Full-Length Camera Inspection Before Replacement

Before recommending full replacement, we run a complete camera inspection along the entire lateral, not just the section that's currently causing symptoms, to confirm the full scope of what needs replacing versus what's still in workable condition.

This inspection also determines whether trenchless replacement is viable for your property's soil and access conditions, or whether traditional excavation is the more practical approach given the pipe's depth and the yard's layout.

Options

Trenchless Replacement vs. Traditional Excavation

Trenchless replacement, using pipe bursting or a similar method, replaces the line through small access points rather than a full trench, which significantly reduces yard disruption and restoration cost. It's a strong option when the existing line's path is clear of major obstructions.

Traditional excavation remains necessary in some cases, particularly where the existing line's path conflicts with other buried utilities or where soil conditions make trenchless methods impractical. We'll assess which applies to your property honestly rather than defaulting to the more expensive option.

Pricing

What Affects Sewer Line Replacement Cost

Cost Factors

  • Method: trenchless replacement generally costs less than full excavation due to reduced restoration.
  • Line length and depth: longer or deeper laterals require more material and labor.
  • Access: obstructions like mature trees or hardscaping can complicate either method.
  • Permits: Everett and Snohomish County require permitting for sewer lateral replacement.
  • Restoration: excavation requires yard restoration that trenchless methods largely avoid.

Sewer line replacement due to age and deterioration is generally not covered by standard homeowner's insurance. A specific sewer line endorsement, if you have one, may provide partial coverage.

Before You Call

What to Ask Before Committing to Replacement

  • Ask to see the full camera inspection footage, not just a verbal summary of findings.
  • Ask specifically why repair isn't sufficient for your line's condition.
  • Confirm whether trenchless replacement is viable for your property before assuming excavation is required.
  • Ask about the permit process and expected timeline for Everett and Snohomish County approval.
  • Get a clear scope of what yard restoration is included if excavation is needed.
Questions

Sewer Line Replacement Questions

How do I know if I need replacement instead of repair?

A full camera inspection showing multiple failure points, a significant belly, or widespread deterioration is the deciding factor, not just how many times the line has backed up.

Is trenchless replacement always possible?

Not always. It depends on the line's path, depth, and any obstructions. We'll confirm during the camera inspection and site assessment.

How long does full replacement take?

Trenchless replacement is often completed in one day. Traditional excavation can take several days depending on line length and restoration needs.

Do I need a permit for sewer line replacement?

Yes, both Everett and Snohomish County require permitting, which we handle as part of the project.

Do you serve areas outside Everett?

Yes, including Mukilteo, Snohomish, Lake Stevens, Mill Creek, and Edmonds.
