Geography

Lynnwood Sits Where I-5 and I-405 Meet

Lynnwood is the fourth-largest city in Snohomish County, with 38,568 residents at the 2020 census. It sits where Interstate 5 and Interstate 405 meet, Highway 99 runs along its west side, and Link light rail reaches the Lynnwood City Center station. Alderwood Mall, which opened in 1979, turned eastern Lynnwood into a retail and office district, while Edmonds College and the Lynnwood Convention Center add institutional and commercial buildings to the mix.

From our Everett base it is about 13 miles by road to the southwest, usually 20 to 25 minutes depending on I-5 traffic. Lynnwood sits between Mukilteo and Mill Creek to the north and Edmonds to the west, so it fills the gap in our service chain between those communities.

Housing

A Postwar Suburb That Is Now Building Up

Lynnwood grew as a postwar suburb along the Pacific Highway without a traditional downtown, and its housing reflects that. Neighborhoods such as Meadowdale, Alderwood Manor, Cedar Valley, Scriber Lake, and Martha Lake each have their own building eras, so a service call can land on a mid-century house with original drain lines one day and a newer townhome the next.

City Center and the Alderwood area are now adding apartments, townhomes, and mixed-use buildings, so shared stacks, tenant coordination, and property-manager work are a regular part of plumbing here alongside single-family service.

Local Signature

Commercial Corridors and the Scriber Creek Low Ground

Retail and restaurants along Highway 99 and around Alderwood Mall, plus the convention center and the college campus, make commercial work a real part of Lynnwood plumbing. Commercial plumbing calls here often involve grease traps, drain maintenance, and backflow testing that keeps a business compliant with the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Washington State.

In the middle of the city, Scriber Lake and Scriber Creek form a natural low point that the city itself uses to hold stormwater at Scriber Lake Park. Properties near that low ground are the ones where we pay closer attention to crawlspace moisture, drainage, and sewer lines that slow down during storms.

Coverage

ZIP 98036, 98037, and 98087: How Lynnwood Splits Up

ZIP 98036 covers the Lynnwood City Center area and, together with 98037, the Alderwood Mall area, so commercial and multifamily work concentrates there. ZIP 98037 and ZIP 98087 also carry the Lynnwood mailing name across more of the city and its surroundings.

ZIP codes are mailing areas rather than city limits, so some Lynnwood-addressed properties, including parts of Martha Lake and Alderwood Manor, sit outside the city itself. We serve them the same way. Our dispatch also reaches the neighboring 98020 and 98026 ZIP codes in Edmonds, 98043 in Mountlake Terrace, 98012 in Mill Creek, and 98208 in Everett, for the same drain, water heater, and commercial work Lynnwood calls for most.

Emergency Prep

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Lynnwood

  • Shut off your main water valve immediately if a pipe bursts, then call.
  • In a townhome, condo, or apartment, shut off your unit's valve and tell your property manager right away, since a shared stack can affect neighbors.
  • Businesses along Highway 99 and near Alderwood Mall: note whether the problem is in a grease trap, a floor drain, or a restroom line when you call.
  • Photograph any water damage for insurance purposes once the water is off.
  • Avoid running water in any fixture that has already started to back up.
  • Have your ZIP code (98036, 98037, or 98087) ready when you call.
