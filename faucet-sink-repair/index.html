Faucet & Sink Repair in Everett, WA

A dripping faucet is more than an annoyance. Left alone, it wastes water continuously and can signal a worn cartridge or valve that gets harder to source the longer it sits.

Call (425) 845-0257 See FAQs

Faucet and sink repair covers a wide set of specific problems: a persistent drip, a leak under the sink at the supply lines or drain connection, low water pressure at a single fixture, or a full fixture replacement when a faucet is outdated or beyond repair. Most of these are diagnosed and fixed in a single visit.

A dripping faucet almost always comes down to a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring, and the exact part depends on the faucet's brand and style, which is why matching the right replacement part matters more than it might seem for a seemingly simple repair.

Local Cause

Common Faucet Issues in Everett Homes

Homes with original faucets from a kitchen or bathroom that hasn't been updated in 15-20 years often have internal cartridges or valve seats worn from mineral deposits and normal use, which is the most common cause of a persistent drip we see regardless of the home's age.

Under-sink leaks at the supply line connections are also common in older homes where the original compression fittings have hardened or corroded over decades, particularly when the supply lines themselves are original galvanized rather than more recent flexible braided lines.

Signs

Signs of a Faucet or Sink Problem

  • A persistent drip from the faucet spout even when fully closed.
  • Water pooling under the sink near the supply lines or drain connection.
  • Reduced water pressure or flow at a single faucet.
  • A loose or wobbling faucet base at the sink deck.
  • Visible corrosion on supply line fittings under the sink.
Method

Diagnosing the Actual Leak Point

For a dripping faucet, we identify the specific worn component, cartridge, washer, valve seat, or O-ring, since faucet designs vary significantly by manufacturer and matching the wrong part leads to a repeat failure.

For an under-sink leak, we check each connection point individually: the supply line fittings, the shutoff valves, and the drain trap assembly, since a leak that seems to be coming from the faucet can actually originate at a different connection entirely.

Options

Repair or Full Fixture Replacement

Most drips and under-sink leaks are resolved with a targeted component repair on the existing fixture. For an older faucet where replacement parts are difficult to source, or where the fixture itself shows broader wear, full replacement is often more cost-effective than continuing to chase individual part failures.

We can also handle a straightforward fixture upgrade if you're simply looking to update an older faucet style, including matching new supply line connections to current standards. If the leak is actually coming from a garbage disposal connection rather than the faucet itself, we'll diagnose that during the same visit.

Pricing

What Affects Faucet & Sink Repair Cost

Cost Factors

  • Repair type: a cartridge or washer replacement costs far less than full fixture replacement.
  • Part availability: some older or discontinued faucet models require sourcing specific parts.
  • Access: a faucet in a tight cabinet or vanity space can add labor time.
  • Supply line condition: corroded fittings may need replacement alongside the faucet repair.
  • Fixture quality: replacement faucet cost varies significantly by brand and finish.

A dripping faucet or minor under-sink leak is a maintenance issue, not typically an insurance matter, unless it results in sudden, significant water damage.

Before You Call

What to Check Before You Call

  • Check under the sink for visible moisture or pooling at the supply lines or drain trap.
  • Note whether the drip is constant or only after the faucet is used.
  • If you can access it, note the faucet's brand, often on the handle or base, to help source parts.
  • Check whether the shutoff valves under the sink still turn, since these are useful in a future emergency.
  • Note if reduced pressure affects one faucet only or the whole house, which changes the likely cause.
Questions

Faucet & Sink Repair Questions

Why does my faucet keep dripping after a repair?

This usually means the wrong replacement part was used, or a second worn component wasn't addressed during the original repair. We diagnose all the likely failure points in one visit.

Is it worth repairing an old faucet or should I replace it?

If parts are readily available and the fixture is otherwise in good condition, repair makes sense. For discontinued models or fixtures showing broader wear, replacement is often more practical.

Can you match my existing sink hardware finish?

In many cases, yes. We'll discuss available options that match or complement your existing fixtures.

Do you handle both kitchen and bathroom faucets?

Yes, both kitchen and bathroom faucet repair and installation.

Do you serve areas outside Everett?

Yes, including Snohomish, Mukilteo, Lake Stevens, Mill Creek, and Edmonds.
