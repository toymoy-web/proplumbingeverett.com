About Everett Pro Plumbing

A local plumbing service built around the specific plumbing problems Everett's housing stock actually produces, not a generic template applied to any city.

Everett Pro Plumbing operates out of Everett, WA, serving Everett proper and the surrounding Snohomish County communities of Mukilteo, Lake Stevens, Snohomish, Mill Creek, and Edmonds. We run true 24/7 emergency dispatch, meaning a call after midnight reaches a real licensed plumber, not an answering service that schedules you for the next business day.

We operate under Washington State's plumbing contractor licensing requirements through the Department of Labor & Industries, the same regulatory framework and licensing standards every legitimate plumbing business in this state works under.

Why We Focus on Local Housing Age

Everett's plumbing problems are shaped heavily by the city's construction history. A large share of homes here were built before 1970, meaning original galvanized steel supply lines and clay sewer laterals are common, not rare. That's the reason our basement waterproofing and repiping pages exist as detailed, dedicated services rather than a single generic "plumbing repair" listing: these are real, high-frequency problems in this specific market, and we've built our diagnostic approach around them.

We treat every service call as a diagnosis problem first. A burst pipe, a flooded basement, or a sewer backup all have multiple possible causes, and finding the actual cause before recommending a fix is what keeps a repair from being repeated a few months later.

What We Don't Do

We don't inflate quotes with vague line items, and we don't recommend a full system replacement when a targeted repair genuinely solves the problem. We also don't operate with fabricated trust signals: we won't claim a specific number of years in business or jobs completed unless we can back it up, and we'd rather tell you honestly what we don't know yet about a problem than guess with confidence we don't have.

Licensing and Compliance

Plumbing work in Washington State requires contractor licensing through the Department of Labor & Industries, and permitting through the relevant city or county for many types of work, including repiping, sewer line replacement, and gas line installation. We handle permitting as part of the projects that require it rather than leaving it to the homeowner to sort out separately.

Our Service Area

We serve Everett and five surrounding Snohomish County communities directly: Mukilteo, Lake Stevens, Snohomish, Mill Creek, and Edmonds. See our full service area page for the complete coverage picture, including neighboring areas we serve without a dedicated page.

How We Approach Every Call

Whether it's a 2 a.m. burst pipe or a scheduled water heater installation, the process starts the same way: understanding what's actually happening before proposing a fix. For an emergency, that means stopping the immediate damage first, then diagnosing the underlying cause so the same problem doesn't recur in a few months. For scheduled work, it means a clear, upfront explanation of the options and their real trade-offs, not just a single number with no context.

We also try to be straightforward about what a repair will and won't fix. If a customer calls about a single leak but the underlying system shows signs of broader failure, we say so directly, along with what that means for cost and timeline, rather than quietly recommending the more expensive option or ignoring the bigger picture to close a smaller job faster.

Working With Property Managers and Businesses

Beyond residential service, we work with property managers and commercial clients throughout our service area, including scheduled maintenance contracts covering grease trap cleaning, backflow testing, and drain maintenance. See our commercial plumbing page for what that looks like in practice.

How We Communicate Pricing

Every service page on this site includes a real breakdown of what affects cost for that specific service, not a vague range with no explanation. We believe a homeowner deciding between a repair and a replacement deserves to understand the actual trade-offs, not just a final number. Free estimates apply to non-emergency work, and for emergency calls we provide upfront pricing before any repair begins.

A Note on What We Publish Online

Every local fact referenced across this site, housing construction eras, specific neighborhood names, historical details, comes from real, verifiable sources: city and county records, Wikipedia, and published local history. We don't fabricate customer testimonials, star ratings, or years-in-business figures to fill out a page. If a claim about our business isn't something we can back up, it doesn't appear on this site.

This applies to pricing information too. Every service page lists real cost factors, access, material, scope, permits, rather than a made-up price range designed to look reassuring without meaning anything specific to your situation.
