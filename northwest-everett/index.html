Everett Neighborhoods

Plumber in Northwest Everett, WA

Northwest Everett is an older residential part of the city. We handle fixture repairs, water heaters, leaks and drain problems in its homes.

Call (425) 845-0257

Where Northwest Everett is

The City of Everett lists Northwest Everett among its neighborhoods, on the northwest side of the city and mostly residential. Some maps place the line between it and Bayside near 19th Street, but these boundaries are not official, and different sources draw them differently.

We confirm your street address when you call. Anything inside the Everett city limits is inside our service area.

Older residential streets

The older residential blocks in this part of the city mean a wide range of home ages and remodel histories. Some houses have been updated top to bottom, while others have a mix of newer fixtures and older supply and drain lines. We do not guess which one you have.

We start by looking at the visible plumbing and testing water pressure and drainage. Then we explain what we see and whether repiping or a smaller repair makes more sense.

Typical calls

Water heaters are a frequent request. When a tank is leaking or the water runs lukewarm, we compare water heater repair with water heater replacement and give you the cost difference before work starts.

Kitchen and bath fixtures are the other regular job. We handle faucet and sink repair and toilet repair, and we look for the small leak behind the cabinet that causes bigger damage later.

Planning around a remodel

If you are remodeling a bathroom or kitchen, the best time to look at old supply and drain lines is while the walls are open. We can look at the plumbing during the project and tell you what is worth replacing. That is often cheaper than opening the wall twice.

Before you call

Have your address ready and a short description of the symptom. If you suspect a hidden leak, check the water meter with all fixtures off. A moving dial points to a leak, and we can help with leak detection.

Before You Call

Northwest Everett Plumbing Questions

What streets count as Northwest Everett?

The City of Everett lists Northwest Everett as a neighborhood, but its lines are not official and maps differ. Call with your address and we will confirm it is inside our service area.

How do I know whether to repair or replace my water heater?

Age, leak location and repair cost all matter. We explain the options and the cost difference before we start any work.

Should I repipe my older home?

Not automatically. We inspect the system first and recommend repiping only if the condition of the pipes justifies it.

How can I tell if I have a hidden leak?

Turn off every fixture, then watch your water meter. If the dial keeps moving, water is going somewhere. Call us and we can trace it.

Do you work during a remodel?

Yes. We can look at supply and drain lines while walls are open and give you a clear scope for any plumbing changes.
