Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Everett Pro Plumbing ("we," "us") collects and uses information from visitors to this website.

Information We Collect

This website is call-only and does not include a contact form. We do not collect personal information through this site beyond standard, anonymized web server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) used for basic site analytics and security.

Information Collected by Phone or Email

If you call or email us to request service, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, address, and a description of the plumbing issue, in order to schedule and perform the requested service. This information is used solely for that purpose and is not sold to third parties.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use standard analytics tools to understand general traffic patterns. These tools do not collect personally identifiable information.

Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to contact@proplumbingeverett.com.
