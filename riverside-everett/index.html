Plumber in Riverside, Everett WA

Riverside is described as the oldest neighborhood in Everett. Our crews handle plumbing in its older homes, duplexes and newer remodels.

Call (425) 845-0257

Where Riverside is

According to Historic Everett, Riverside sits on the east side of Everett between Pacific Avenue and 19th Street, and between Broadway and the Snohomish River. That page describes it as the oldest neighborhood in the city, with development starting in 1892 after the Swalwell and Mitchell plats were filed.

Interstate 5 was built through Riverside in the early 1960s and split it into two halves. These are the boundaries one local history source uses. They are not official lines, so we confirm your address when you call.

What the housing history means for plumbing

The same source says most Riverside homes date from 1892 or from building booms between 1900 and 1910 and in the mid-1920s. It also mentions Queen Anne houses, early duplexes and a set of identical houses built in 1918. It does not say what pipes are inside them.

In general, a house this old has had decades of repairs and remodels, so the plumbing is often a mix of materials and ages. We do not assume what is in the walls. We look at the fixtures, the shutoff valves and the access points first, then explain what we find before recommending repiping or smaller fixes.

Historic overlay zoning

Historic Everett says parts of Riverside fall inside a Historic Overlay zone, which uses zoning guidelines to keep the historic character of the neighborhood. We cannot tell you how it applies to a given property. If a project may be affected, check with the City of Everett before you schedule it.

Common plumbing calls in older neighborhoods

Slow drains, recurring clogs and sewer backups are a common reason older-home owners call. We start with drain cleaning and a camera look when the clog keeps coming back, then discuss sewer line repair only if the line itself is the problem.

Hidden leaks and old water heaters are the other regular calls. Our team uses leak detection tools to trace a leak before opening a wall, and we cover water heater repair and water heater replacement for tanks that have reached the end of their life.

Before you call

It helps to know where your main shutoff valve is and whether the home has had a sewer line inspection. Have your street address ready and a short description of the symptom. Photos of the fixture or the water meter help us plan the right tools.

Source for the local history above: Historic Everett, Riverside Neighborhood self-guided history tour. This is a local reference and not an official City record.

Riverside Plumbing Questions

Is Riverside really the oldest part of Everett?

Historic Everett describes Riverside as the oldest neighborhood in the city, dating to 1892. We cite that source for the history only. It is a local history page, not a City record.

Does the age of my Riverside home tell you what pipes it has?

No. Age gives us a reason to look carefully, but remodels and repairs change what is in a house. We confirm the actual materials during the visit and do not guess from the build year.

Does the Historic Overlay affect plumbing repairs inside my house?

Historic Everett describes the overlay as a set of zoning guidelines that keep the historic character of the neighborhood. We cannot say how it applies to your property, so ask the City of Everett before any project that might be affected.

Do you serve both sides of I-5 in Riverside?

Yes. We serve addresses inside Everett on either side of the freeway. Give us your street address on the phone so we can confirm it.

Can you inspect my sewer line before I buy a Riverside home?

Yes. A camera inspection of the sewer line is a common step for older homes. Call and tell us the address and your timeline.
