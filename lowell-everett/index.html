Plumber in Lowell, Everett WA

Lowell is a riverside neighborhood with a history older than Everett itself. We service its modest older homes and the drainage questions that come with low ground.

About Lowell

HistoryLink says Lowell dates to 1863, when Eugene Smith set up a logging camp there, nearly 30 years before Everett was founded. The community was platted in 1873 and was later annexed into Everett in the 1960s. The source does not give an exact annexation year.

Lowell lies on the west bank of the Snohomish River, south of 41st Street in Everett. The same source describes it today as a residential area with modest homes, some dating to the 1890s and early 1900s.

Low ground and drainage

Neighborhoods near a river can have wetter ground than neighborhoods on a hill. That is general information, not a finding about any specific Lowell property. If your crawlspace, basement or garage stays damp, the cause could be groundwater, surface drainage or a plumbing leak.

We separate those causes. We check supply lines and drains for leaks first, then discuss sump pump service or basement waterproofing if the water is coming from the ground.

Interstate 5 and the old street grid

HistoryLink notes that I-5 was built through Lowell in the 1960s and removed dozens of homes. The original plat used small 60 by 120 foot lots. Small lots can mean tight spacing between the house, the sewer connection and neighboring utilities, which we keep in mind when we plan digging.

Services that fit Lowell homes

Many calls here involve drains and sewer lines. We start with drain cleaning, and if a camera shows damage or a belly in the pipe, we talk through sewer line repair options and how much of the line would need work.

We also handle water heater repair, faucet and sink repair and leak detection for the older homes that have built up small problems over the years.

Before you call

Note where the water shows up and when. Does it appear after rain, or does it show up in dry weather too? That one detail often tells us whether to bring leak detection gear or pump equipment.

Source for the local history above: HistoryLink, Everett Neighborhoods: Lowell, Thumbnail History. This is a local reference and not an official City record.

Before You Call

Lowell Plumbing Questions

Is Lowell inside the City of Everett?

Yes. HistoryLink says Lowell was annexed into Everett in the 1960s. We serve addresses inside the city limits, and we confirm your exact address on the call.

My Lowell basement or crawlspace gets wet. Is that a plumbing problem?

It can be a plumbing leak, but it can also be groundwater or surface drainage. We check for leaks first, then explain whether a sump pump or waterproofing would be the better next step.

Do you install sump pumps in Lowell?

Yes. We install and repair sump pumps, and we look at where the water is coming from before choosing equipment.

Will you dig up my yard to fix a sewer problem?

Not unless it is needed. A camera inspection shows what the line looks like. We then explain the options and the scope before any digging starts.

Are Lowell homes all old?

HistoryLink says some Lowell homes date to the 1890s and early 1900s. We do not assume the age or pipe type of a home, and we look at each house on its own.
