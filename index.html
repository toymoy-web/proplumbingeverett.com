Safety First

Before You Call: What to Check in a Plumbing Emergency

  • Locate your main water shutoff valve now, before an emergency, so you're not searching for it during one.
  • If a pipe bursts, shut off the main valve first, then call, since stopping the water matters more than the callback speed.
  • For a suspected gas smell near a water heater or gas line, leave the house and call from outside before calling us.
  • Photograph any standing water or visible damage for your insurance company before cleanup begins.
  • Note when the problem started and whether it's gotten worse, since that timeline helps with an accurate phone diagnosis.
Pricing

What Affects Your Plumbing Repair Cost in Everett

Cost Factors

  • Access: a slab leak or a line under a finished basement costs more to reach than an exposed crawlspace pipe.
  • Pipe material: replacing galvanized steel or cast iron typically costs more than working on existing copper or PEX.
  • Scope: a single fixture repair is priced differently than a whole-home repipe or a full sewer lateral replacement.
  • Timing: after-hours dispatch carries a different rate than a scheduled daytime appointment.
  • Permits: Everett and Snohomish County permitting requirements apply to certain repiping and sewer work.

Most homeowner's policies cover sudden, accidental water damage, like a burst pipe, but not gradual leaks or wear-related failure. Check your policy's specific language before assuming a repair is covered.
