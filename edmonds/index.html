The Bowl's Century-Old Pipes Meet Steep Hillside Drainage

Edmonds' historic downtown, known locally as the Bowl, sits close to the Edmonds Ferry Terminal and carries homes dating to the early 1900s, the same pre-1950s galvanized and cast-iron pipe profile we see in Everett's oldest neighborhoods and in Mukilteo's Old Town. What's specific to Edmonds is the hillside geography surrounding the Bowl: steep lots where drainage and foundation work compound whatever plumbing repair is already needed.

Housing

Waterfront Bowl vs. Hillside Edmonds

For Bowl-area homes, that means repiping and sewer line repair come up frequently, the same pattern as Everett's own century-old blocks. For the hillside neighborhoods above the Bowl, slab leak detection and basement waterproofing see more real demand, since slope and drainage add a mechanical stress factor that flat-lot Everett properties don't face in the same way. A slab leak on a sloped hillside lot also tends to travel further before surfacing, which is one more reason acoustic detection matters more here than on a flat property.

ZIP 98020 and 98026: The Bowl vs. the Hillside

ZIP 98020 covers the downtown Bowl and waterfront, where repairs to pre-1950s galvanized and cast-iron plumbing follow the same Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) replacement standards we apply in Everett's oldest blocks. ZIP 98026 extends into the surrounding hillside neighborhoods, where slab leak and drainage-related calls are more common.

Both ZIPs receive the same 24/7 emergency dispatch coverage from our Everett base, and we bring both acoustic leak detection and standard repair equipment to every Edmonds call given the mix of housing types across the two ZIPs.

We also dispatch north into the neighboring 98275 (Mukilteo) ZIP and east into 98036 (Lynnwood), extending the same basement waterproofing and repiping coverage beyond Edmonds proper.

Emergency Prep

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Edmonds

  • Shut off your main water valve immediately for any burst pipe, then call.
  • Bowl-area homes near the ferry terminal: original galvanized pipe is common, plan repairs accordingly.
  • Hillside properties: watch for warm spots on flooring, which can indicate a slab leak on sloped lots.
  • Photograph any water damage for insurance purposes once the water is off.
  • Note whether you're in the downtown Bowl or a hillside neighborhood, since the likely cause differs.
  • If you're on a steep lot, also note any recent drainage or grading changes nearby, since that context helps with diagnosis.

Before You Call

Edmonds Plumbing Questions

Is emergency service available in Edmonds?

Yes, including for burst pipes in the historic Bowl and slab leaks on the surrounding hillside properties.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Edmonds?

Yes, we cover both 98020 and 98026, which together serve the downtown Bowl and the surrounding hillside and residential areas.

Is the Bowl's plumbing really that old?

Many homes in Edmonds' downtown Bowl date to the early 1900s and commonly still carry original galvanized supply lines, similar to Everett's own oldest neighborhoods.

Why do hillside homes see more slab leaks?

Sloped lots add mechanical stress and drainage complexity that flat properties don't face, which is a real contributing factor alongside normal pipe aging, and it's why we check foundation grading alongside the plumbing itself on a hillside service call.

How far is Edmonds from your Everett office?

About 12 miles south, within our regular daily service area, though the farthest of the five communities we've built dedicated coverage pages for.

Do you handle ferry-terminal-adjacent properties differently?

Not structurally, though homes right along the waterfront near the terminal tend to be among the oldest in the Bowl, so we come prepared for original galvanized and cast-iron infrastructure by default rather than discovering it partway through a job.
