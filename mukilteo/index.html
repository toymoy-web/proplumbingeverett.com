Mukilteo's Split Housing Stock: Old Town vs. the Bluff

Mukilteo's plumbing calls split cleanly along the city's own geography. Old Town, built up around the 1906 lighthouse before 1950, still runs original galvanized supply lines and clay drain tile in a lot of its waterfront homes. The Harbour Pointe area and the newer subdivisions on the bluff above, developed mostly in the 1970s through 1990s after the Boeing Everett plant opened nearby, are on copper and PVC and see a different, newer-system set of calls.

What This Means for Mukilteo Plumbing Calls

For Old Town addresses, that means repiping and sewer line repair come up more often than in the newer parts of the city, since the original pre-1950s infrastructure is now well past its service life. Newer bluff-area homes near Harbour Pointe see more calls tied to modern systems reaching their own service-life milestones: water heaters, disposals, and fixture upgrades rather than old-pipe replacement.
